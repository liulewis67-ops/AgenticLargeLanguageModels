## Part I Language Modeling

{1}------------------------------------------------

![](assets/_page_1_Picture_0.jpeg)

{2}------------------------------------------------

In this first part, we discuss the basics of language modeling and machine learning. We begin with the principles of representing language using tokens and embeddings. We then discuss attention, which underlies the Transformer architecture and the success of large language models. We also review two important machine-learning techniques: supervised learning and reinforcement learning. The next chapter makes these ideas more concrete by walking through a typical training pipeline, from pretraining and fine-tuning to inference, and by introducing common benchmarks. The final content chapter examines the *large* in large language models: how performance scales with parameter count, training data, and compute, and how efficient models can benefit from scale while controlling costs. The part concludes with a hands-on case study in which you will put these ideas together by building a small language model from scratch.
