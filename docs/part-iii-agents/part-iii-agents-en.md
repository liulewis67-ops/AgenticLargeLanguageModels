## Part III Agents

{1}------------------------------------------------

![](assets/_page_1_Picture_0.jpeg)

{2}------------------------------------------------

We have now covered the foundations of LLMs and agents: how language and tokenization work, how LLMs are trained and fine-tuned, how advanced reasoning models reason and self-reflect, and how learning extends across language, vision, and action. We have also examined the intricacies of metalearning.

We now turn to the core of agentic LLM research: the different interaction styles of LLM agents. This part contains one content chapter and one practical case study. We first review the five interaction styles introduced so far and discuss the importance of memory. The case study then brings together many of these techniques as you build a self-reflective LLM agent that can play AI games such as TextWorld and NetHack.
