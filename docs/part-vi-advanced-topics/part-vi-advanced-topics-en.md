## Part VI Advanced Topics

{1}------------------------------------------------

![](assets/_page_1_Picture_0.jpeg)

{2}------------------------------------------------

Agentic LLMs are a new field with active research across many topics. This part highlights four areas with important ongoing developments: synthetic data generation, advanced architectures, mechanistic interpretability, and cognitive perspectives on agents. Many other research directions are worth exploring; we selected these topics because they connect to questions raised earlier in the book.

The previous chapters aimed to explain their material as clearly as possible, at a level suitable for a master's course. The next chapters cover more advanced material, at a level suitable for a master's thesis. We still aim for clarity, but move faster and go into greater depth, with more references to research papers. We encourage you to consult these sources, and hope the chapters offer inspiration for your own research.
