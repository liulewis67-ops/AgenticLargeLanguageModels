# Source and translation notes

This unofficial reading edition is based on [Agentic Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13455), by Max van Duijn, Michiel van der Meer, Aske Plaat, and Niki van Stein. Refer to the [original arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13455).

The English pages are extracted and formatted from the source PDF. The Chinese pages are this project's translations and are **pending human review**. Extraction, formulas, formatting, links, and translation may contain errors. The paper page currently links to [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); retain the attribution found in the source.

Each reading page displays the source and physical PDF page range. Use “View source Markdown” to trace the corresponding repository file. To report a problem, open an issue and include the chapter, language, page, and suggested correction.
